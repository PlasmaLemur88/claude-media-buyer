# Mad Libs: build on a structure that already worked

**The fastest way to a good ad is not a blank page.** Take an ad that already
went viral, keep its beat structure and timings exactly, and swap in your own
subject. The rhythm is the format; the content is the variable.

Below is one worked example end to end: the Opal screen-time ad, transcribed
frame by frame, broken into its beats, turned into a fill-in-the-blank
template, and then filled in for a placeholder brand called Example: a
fictional company that teaches people to use AI at work, with a free weekly
guide and a paid course.

**How to use this with an operator:**

1. Ask what they want the ad to be about.
2. Find the beat structure that fits, or use the one below.
3. Fill the blanks WITH them, out loud, one beat at a time. Do not write the
   whole thing and present it.
4. Then route the finished script into a format: `OneWord`, `Cards`, or a
   generated-footage cut.

**Do not copy the source ad's footage, script or shots.** The structure is the
reusable part. The words and pictures have to be ours.

# Worked example: the Opal ad, as a template for Example

Source: https://www.instagram.com/p/C0cF7LQNqL4/ (Opal screen-time app, 51s, 9:16, faceless).
Watched frame by frame and transcribed. To study it yourself, download it with the `video-downloader` skill and watch it with `gemini-video`.

## 2. Beat-by-beat visual map

1. **Cold open, 0-1s.** Real footage of the mundane object (iPhone in an Apple Store). VO: "This is not an [object]."
2. **The reframe barrage, 1-9s.** ~1 cut per second: NYT "$3 Trillion" headline, tank cutaway diagram, WWII tank footage, brain MRI sheets, anatomy model, hypnotic phone-screen tunnel, brain overlaid on a scrolling face, "HIPPOCAMPUS" diagram. Every cut is *evidence* for the scary reframe.
3. **"This is about YOU," 9-14s.** A person doomscrolling in bed, a real iOS Screen Time screenshot (6h 33m), TikTok scroll over a shoulder, extreme closeup of an eye with the feed reflected in it. The viewer sees themselves.
4. **Authority borrow, 14-21s.** Tristan Harris congressional testimony + talking-head footage, huge caption overlay "AN INVISIBLE ASYMMETRY OF POWER", name card "TRISTAN HARRIS". Credibility on loan, no host needed.
5. **Gut-punch title cards, 20-25s.** Black screens, white type: "THE BOTTOM LINE" / "YOU ARE BEING [harvested]" (intercut with a horror-film shot) / "AND NO ONE IS COMING TO SAVE YOU." Then the turn: "But all hope is not lost."
6. **The physical metaphor, 25-42s.** Tabletop, gloved hands, quarters = hours. Two columns of coins: "24 HOURS IN TODAY". Coins dropped into mason jars labeled "SLEEP EAT SH*T", "HOPES & DREAMS", "CHERISHED MEMORIES": and one jar stuffed to the brim, plastered with TikTok/IG/FB/Twitter/Snapchat/YouTube stickers. The labeled jars sit nearly empty next to it.
7. **The smash, 43-48s.** Hammer through the social-media jar, slow-mo glass and coins everywhere. Catharsis = the product's job, shown physically.
8. **Anti-CTA + product reveal, 49-51s.** Title card: "NOW GET THE F*** OFF OF INSTAGRAM." Cut to the actual product screen: "Instagram is blocked by Opal." Logo. End.

**Why it works:** mundane object -> menacing reframe with rapid-fire "evidence" -> mirror held up to the viewer -> borrowed authority -> despair floor ("no one is coming") -> hope pivot -> a physical, tactile metaphor for an abstract cost -> violent catharsis -> an anti-CTA that *is* the product demo. The ad never asks you to buy; it tells you to leave, and the product is how you leave.

## 3. The Mad Libs template

Fill every [BLANK]. Keep the beat lengths; the rhythm is the format.

> **BEAT 1: Cold open (0-1s).** Footage of [MUNDANE OBJECT the audience touches daily].
> VO: "This is not [MUNDANE OBJECT]."
>
> **BEAT 2: Reframe barrage (1-8s).** VO: "This is [GIANT NUMBER] [MENACING REFRAME] aimed straight at your [VULNERABLE THING]." Visuals: 6-8 rapid cuts of real-looking evidence: [HEADLINE SCREENSHOT], [TECHNICAL DIAGRAM], [ARCHIVAL FOOTAGE], [SCIENTIFIC IMAGERY].
>
> **BEAT 3: Mirror (8-14s).** VO: "If you're watching this, it's because [THE MECHANISM: how the villain exploits the viewer's own impulses] to get you to give up [THE PRECIOUS RESOURCE]." Visuals: someone who looks like the viewer doing the guilty behavior + one undeniable screenshot receipt ([THE VIEWER'S OWN DAMNING STAT]).
>
> **BEAT 4: Authority borrow (14-20s).** VO: "This is [AUTHORITY'S KEY PHRASE]. [AUTHORITY NAME] is the foremost expert on this: [WHERE TO VERIFY]. But the bottom line is this:" Visuals: real footage of the authority, name card, key phrase as giant type.
>
> **BEAT 5: Gut punch (20-25s).** Black title cards: "THE BOTTOM LINE" / "YOU ARE BEING [VISCERAL VERB]" / "AND NO ONE IS COMING TO SAVE YOU." Then: "But all hope is not lost. Hear me out."
>
> **BEAT 6: Physical metaphor (25-41s).** VO: "[UNIVERSAL TRUTH about the precious resource]... but how much of it goes to [THE VILLAIN]?" Visuals: [TANGIBLE PROP] = [ABSTRACT RESOURCE], sorted into labeled containers; the villain's container is overflowing, the containers labeled [WHAT THE VIEWER ACTUALLY WANTS] sit empty.
>
> **BEAT 7: Catharsis (41-48s).** [DESTRUCTION OR LIBERATION of the villain's container], slow motion.
>
> **BEAT 8: Anti-CTA (48-52s).** Title card: "NOW [COMMAND THAT SOUNDS AGAINST OUR INTEREST]." Cut to [PRODUCT SCREEN doing exactly that command]. Logo.

## 4. Filled in for Example's two biggest paying personas (Founders and Individuals; for a real brand, the shares come from its `BRAND-GUIDE.md`)

Working title: **"A System vs. a Feed."** ORGANIC post, not an ad. Stays beat-for-beat on the Opal structure: same menace, same mirror, same jars, same anti-CTA. The threat is AI aimed at how you make money; the asymmetry is between people working from a system and people working from a feed. The video never sells; the free-guide invite lives in the caption.

- [MUNDANE OBJECT] -> the ChatGPT icon on a home screen
- [GIANT NUMBER + MENACING REFRAME] -> "a four trillion dollar machine pointed straight at the way you make money"
- [PRECIOUS RESOURCE] -> your working hours
- [ASYMMETRY] -> "A SYSTEM VS. A FEED"
- [VISCERAL CARD] -> "YOU ARE BEING LAPPED"
- [TANGIBLE PROP] -> quarters = your 24 hours; jars = where they go
- [CATHARSIS] -> the "work a machine can do" jar smashed
- [ANTI-CTA] -> "NOW GET OFF INSTAGRAM." -> "AND GO BUILD SOMETHING." -> end. Invite lives in the caption, not the video.

### Shot-by-shot script (~50s)

**BEAT 1 (0-1s).** Macro: a thumb hovering over the ChatGPT icon on a phone home screen.
VO: **"This is not a chatbot."**

**BEAT 2 (1-8s).** Rapid evidence cuts, 1/sec: NVIDIA $4T market-cap headline; data-center aisles; a robot arm; an org chart; a hiring page with "AI required" in the listing.
VO: **"This is a four trillion dollar machine pointed straight at the way you make money."**

**BEAT 3 (8-14s).** The mirror: someone like the viewer at a laptop at night, 40 tabs open. Screenshot receipt: a LinkedIn post: "I automated my whole workflow": 8,412 likes. A competitor's homepage with a "Now AI-powered" banner.
VO: **"If you're watching this, you've felt it. Every week a new model, a new tool, someone shipping your month's work in a day."**

**BEAT 4 (14-20s).** Headline montage, then giant type over it: "A SYSTEM" / "VS. A FEED".
VO: **"They are not smarter than you. They have a system. You have a feed."**

**BEAT 5 (20-25s).** Black cards, white type: "THE BOTTOM LINE" / "YOU ARE BEING LAPPED" / "AND NO ONE IS COMING TO CATCH YOU UP." Beat.
VO: **"You are being lapped, and no one is coming to catch you up. But all hope is not lost. Hear me out."**

**BEAT 6 (25-41s).** Tabletop, gloved hands, quarters in two columns: "24 HOURS IN TODAY" (identical to source). Coins drop into jars: "MEETINGS. EMAIL. ADMIN." (stuffed), "FIGURING OUT AI ALONE" (coins trickling in), "THE WORK ONLY YOU CAN DO" (nearly empty).
VO: **"We all get the same 24 hours. The people winning with AI aren't finding more. They're feeding fewer to work a machine can already do. How many of yours go where they don't have to?"**

**BEAT 7 (41-48s).** Hammer through the "MEETINGS. EMAIL. ADMIN." jar. Slow-mo coins and glass. The "THE WORK ONLY YOU CAN DO" jar stands in the light.
VO: **"Take those hours back."**

**BEAT 8 (48-52s).** Title card: **"NOW GET OFF INSTAGRAM."** Beat. Card: **"AND GO BUILD SOMETHING."** Hold on the standing jar. Small Example wordmark, nothing else.
VO: **"Now get off Instagram. Go build something."**

### Caption (carries the invite; the video stays clean)

> The people winning with AI aren't smarter. They have a system.
> Every week we explain one real AI build, start to finish, free: example.com.
> If you'd rather build alongside people doing the same, the paid course is open too.

### Production notes

- Fully faceless: macro phone shots, stock/archival cuts, title cards, tabletop props. The jar scene is one tabletop shoot or a Kling sequence (hands and a tabletop are well within what Kling handles).
- "$4 trillion" pegs to NVIDIA's market cap; keep the headline screenshot in Beat 2 so the number has its receipt on screen.
- No profanity anywhere. The anti-CTA card does the shock work.
- Organic post: no product screen, no offer in the video. The signup invite is caption-only, so the video earns shares on its own.
- If it performs organically, the paid variant already exists: swap Beat 7 VO back to "This is what Example is for. The guide is free. The course hands you the finished build." and end on the paid screen. Organic tests the hook cheap, paid scales the winner.
- Get the brand owner's sign-off before posting ("get off Instagram" posted on Instagram; the source ran the same irony successfully).

Two more notes carried over from an earlier variant of this script:

- Keep any statistic sourced in the caption so the claim survives comment-section
  scrutiny.
- The anti-CTA must sting for a beat before the resolve card. Do not soften it
  by showing the logo early.
