This brand's files. brand.json and configs.mjs refer to them as assets/<path>
(brand.json) or brand-active/<path> (configs.mjs).

  fonts/   font files with a licence that allows use in ads (SIL OFL is safe)
  logos/   the brand mark, wordmarks, partner logos (transparent PNG or SVG)
  audio/   voiceovers and music beds for this brand's ads
  words/   word timings from scripts/timings.py, one <number>.json per voiceover
  img/     artwork the Cards format shows
