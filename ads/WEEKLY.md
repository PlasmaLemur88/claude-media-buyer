You are running the weekly review for a Meta ad campaign, from inside this folder, as a scheduled task or on "weekly review". You start with no memory: read `ads/CAMPAIGN.md`, `ads/TEST-PLAN.md`, `ads/ANGLES.md`, all of `ads/LOG.md`, and `ads/LEARNINGS.md` if it exists. Work only on ad_account_id and campaign_id from `ads/CAMPAIGN.md`. If Pipeboard is missing or returns an auth error, say so in the chat (and Slack, if set up) and stop. Every date is in account_timezone.

W1. Reconcile. Read the last 7 days of results from Pipeboard at the campaign level. Then read the real count for the same days from the destination's own platform: Substack (new free subscribers), Shopify or Stripe (orders), a CRM (leads), through whichever connector is connected. If none is, ask the operator for the number in one line and carry on without it. Write `ads/checks/reconcile-<date>.md`: Meta's count, the platform's count, the ratio, and one line on what it means (Meta usually counts more; a ratio under 0.5 means the campaign is being judged on a number that is not real).

W2. Analyze by element. At the ad level for the round so far, read impressions, 3-second video views, thruplays, clicks, results, spend, frequency. For each ad compute hook rate (3-second views over impressions), hold (thruplays over 3-second views), CTR, cost per result. Roll each ad up to its angle (from its brief). Call every test by the rules in `ads/TEST-PLAN.md`: scale candidate, kill, or one more round. Name which element won where a hook version beat its siblings.

W3. Audit the account. Run the `ads-meta` skill (`.claude/skills/ads-meta/SKILL.md`) over this campaign and the account it sits in. Its catalog covers the pixel and the Conversions API, attribution, creative diversity and tiring, account structure, audiences, placements, automation, budgets and policy. Write `ads/checks/audit-<date>.md`: one line per control you could evaluate (its id, pass, fail, unknown or not applicable, severity, what you saw, what to do), then the controls you could not evaluate and why. Every fail that carries a recommendation becomes a decision in W6. The audit changes nothing in the account, and anything that touches money still goes through `ads/rules.py` and the operator.

W4. Learnings. Append to `ads/LEARNINGS.md` under today's date: what won and why (the hook, the angle, the format, with its numbers), what lost and the likely reason, the Meta-to-real ratio, the audit's fails, the two or three hypotheses for next round in one line each. Update each angle's status in `ads/ANGLES.md` (`won`, `lost`, `testing`, `untested`). Never invent a number; an element you could not read is "not read".

W5. Next round. Run `ads/STRATEGY.md` from S3, reading `ads/LEARNINGS.md` first: adjust or add angles, write the next round's section in `ads/TEST-PLAN.md`, ending with the line `round <N> approved: pending`, and its briefs. In approval mode, show the plan and wait for "approved", then change that line to `round <N> approved: yes`; in autopilot write `yes` yourself, and the daily check-in builds the briefs one a day. Then set `round: <N>` and `round_started: <today>` in `ads/CAMPAIGN.md`. The check-in builds only briefs whose round carries the `yes` line.

W6. Report. In the chat and on Slack if set up, under 15 lines: the header line from `python3 ads/status.py --short`, the reconcile line, the audit's fails in one line, three lines on what won and lost, the decisions (kills, scale candidates, next round's angles), and the reply line ("say 'approved', 'make them', or 'show learnings'"). Log a "Weekly" entry in `ads/LOG.md`.
